# China-Friendly AI Full-Stack Hiring Memo

AI startup teams hiring remote engineers from China should evaluate async written communication, ownership of ambiguous product slices, and the ability to ship across frontend, backend, and AI provider boundaries.

The strongest signals for this demo are:

- RAG and vector retrieval implementation.
- Provider abstraction for local, Jina, OpenAI, or DeepSeek paths.
- Citations, chunk ids, retrieval scores, and audit logs.
- Docker-based local deployment that keeps recording cost low.
- Frontend workflow design that makes ingestion, query, and debugging visible.

English speaking fluency is useful, but written English collaboration can be enough for async engineering teams when decisions, blockers, and delivery notes are documented clearly.
